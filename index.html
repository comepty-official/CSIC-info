What is CSIC?

Not a chatbot. A doer.

An AI that actually builds things

CSIC — Comepty Super Intelligence Connection — is an autonomous assistant that lives on your Windows computer. You tell it what you want in plain English and it writes the files, installs what's needed, and runs it for you. Websites, games, tools, scripts. You watch, it works.

Made for people who don't code

You never touch a terminal, never install a thing, never read an error message. CSIC handles the technical side and explains what it did in normal words. If something breaks, it fixes it and tells you plainly.

⚡ Does the work

Runs commands, creates files, starts servers — all on its own.

🪟 Windows first

Built and tested for Windows 10 and 11. Other systems come later.

🔒 Stays on your PC

Your projects are saved in folders on your own machine.

The risks — read this first

CSIC can run commands and change files on your computer. That power is real, and you should know exactly what it means before you install it.

  • !
    It can change your computer CSIC creates, edits and deletes files, and runs terminal commands. Always read what it says it's about to do before saying yes.
  • !
    AI can be confidently wrong It can write code that looks right but isn't, or misunderstand your request. Check important results yourself.
  • !
    Only run it on a machine you own Do not install CSIC on a work, school or shared computer without permission from whoever manages it.
  • !
    Back up anything you care about Before letting CSIC work inside an important folder, make a copy somewhere safe.
  • !
    It is not a replacement for your own judgement CSIC follows your instructions. It is not responsible for what you ask it to do, and it won't stop you from asking for something unwise.
